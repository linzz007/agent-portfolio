# Global Runtime Rules

- Every high-priority conclusion must cite both a policy clause and a company fact.
- Do not infer deadlines, subsidy amounts, issuer authority, or eligibility conditions when they are not present in the source.
- Treat Company Wiki as the long-term source of truth for enterprise facts.
- Treat raw policy documents as the source of truth for policy facts.
- SQLite memory can store reports, corrections, preferences, and audit history, but it must not silently replace Company Wiki.
- Any Company Wiki update must be represented as a correction proposal before it is applied.
- P0/P1 items require stronger evidence than P2/P3/P4 items.
