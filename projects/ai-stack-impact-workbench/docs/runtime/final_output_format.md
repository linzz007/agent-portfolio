# Final Output Format

The weekly report should contain:

1. Company context summary.
2. Recent policy overview.
3. P0/P1 priority items first.
4. Impact level, impact type, score, and reasoning for each item.
5. Policy evidence and company evidence for each item.
6. Missing company fields that need user confirmation.
7. Recommended next action.
8. Audit summary with unsupported claim rate and review warnings.

Forbidden output:

- Claims that the company will definitely receive subsidy or approval.
- Legal, tax, or government filing conclusions without human review.
- Silent Company Wiki modification.
- Generated policy facts that are not grounded in the raw policy document.
